## Why Daily Jobs Are Useful

Daily scheduling is the most common setup in production environments. It ensures:  

- **Freshness** — every day’s data gets validated.  
- **Consistency** — anomalies are detected early before they propagate downstream.  
- **Automation** — no need to manually trigger inspections.
